LIBREWING

An interactive experience connecting a WebGL-based mechanical bird editor with a VR world.

YEAR 2026
PLATFORM WebGL / Meta Quest 3
TECHNOLOGY Unity / C# / Node.js / SQLite / OpenXR
ROLE Game Design / Programming / Server / VR

01 / CONCEPT

Create.
Connect.
Control.

LibreWing explores the relationship between players and virtual creatures through the creation and control of a mechanical bird.

Players first create their own bird through a WebGL-based interface. The bird can then be brought into a VR environment and controlled through hand gestures.

02 / WEBGL

Create your bird.

Players can customize their mechanical bird through the web interface, including its engine, skill and appearance.

03 / VR

Control with your body.

In VR, players can interact with the mechanical bird through hand tracking and gestures instead of traditional controllers.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Unity C# WebGL Node.js SQLite OpenXR VR AI
